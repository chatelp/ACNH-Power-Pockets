Send a list

to Power Pockets on your Switch

Add a catalogue.ac list to the Catalog on your console.

  1. 4 digits, on the Receive screen.

  2. Choose the list

    On catalogue.ac, the menu's Export gives a file. A list's Share button gives a link.

  3. Send it

This page comes from your Switch, on your own network. Nothing goes through the internet, and your save is not changed: the list only appears in the Catalog.
